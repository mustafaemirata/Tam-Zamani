FAQ

Is the game free?

Yes. You play with 5 lives a day, and one refills every 4 hours 48 minutes. You can earn lives with ads, or buy coins and PRO.

Can I cheat with my phone's stopwatch?

No. The target appears in the last second of the countdown, the timer hides during the round and the music stops.

Do shop items make the game easier?

Cosmetics only change the look. Timer Glasses and Streak Shields are small helpers; they never tap for you.

How do I duel a friend?

On the Duel tab, create a duel, play it and send the code. Your friend enters it with Kod gir and plays the same target.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your avatar and use Hesabımı sil (Delete my account) at the bottom of your profile.
